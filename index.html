KANSEI

drift in X3D · Babylon WebGPU

A night-drift racing game with four cars and five circuits. Every world in it is an X3D file, and the physics is a separate deterministic simulation, so the same recorded lap plays back identically in any renderer. This page runs it in Babylon.js 9.32.0 in WebGPU mode, through a small X3D reader.

Play v1.1
Keyboard: W gas, S brake, A D steer, Space handbrake, Shift boost, C camera, M sound. Gamepad and touch work too.

What you need

A WebGPU browserChrome or Edge 113 or later, Safari 26, Firefox 141 or later. If it says no adapter answered, the game tells you.
Windows with an Intel UHD 620 or similarChrome may block the GPU for WebGPU. Open chrome://flags/#enable-unsafe-webgpu, set it to Enabled, relaunch. That fixed it on one such machine.
Timing buttonA small "Timing" pill at the lower left, off until you press it. It measures about 20 seconds of frame times on your machine, shows p50, p95 and p99 and a block you can copy. Nothing is uploaded.

What has been checked

  • The simulation hash matches the original X_ITE page on 6 of 6 recorded views, so both pictures show the same car state on the same circuit.
  • The Babylon picture scores 24.4 to 32.8 dB PSNR against X_ITE 16.5.1 on those views (software adapter, 640 x 360). That is correctness evidence, not a speed claim.
  • No frame rate from a real GPU has been published yet. The Timing button is how those numbers get collected, by whoever chooses to press it.
  • The sound is the game's own synthesiser, unchanged from the original.
